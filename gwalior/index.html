Good to know

Which offices handle filings from Madhya Pradesh?

Almost all of these filings are made online. This is where applications from Madhya Pradesh are processed.

Patents
Mumbai Patent Office (appropriate office for Madhya Pradesh)
Trademarks
Trade Marks Registry, Mumbai (appropriate office for Madhya Pradesh)
Designs
Design Wing of the Patent Office, Kolkata
Copyright
Copyright Office, New Delhi
Companies & LLPs
Registrar of Companies, Gwalior (covers Madhya Pradesh)
Shops & establishments
Madhya Pradesh Shops and Establishments Act, 1958
GST
GST portal; Madhya Pradesh State GST and Central GST jurisdictions

Leegtech is a private company and is not affiliated with any of these offices. Official fees are charged at actuals.

Which patent office handles applications from Madhya Pradesh?

Patent applications are filed with the appropriate office of the Indian Patent Office based on the applicant's place of residence or business. For applicants in Madhya Pradesh, that is the Mumbai Patent Office. Filing is done online.

Learn more

Which Trade Marks Registry covers Madhya Pradesh?

For applicants whose principal place of business is in Madhya Pradesh, the appropriate office is the Trade Marks Registry, Mumbai. Applications are filed online.

Learn more

Where are companies in Madhya Pradesh registered?

Companies and LLPs with a registered office in Madhya Pradesh fall under the Registrar of Companies, Gwalior. Incorporation and annual filings are made online on the MCA portal.

Learn more

Do I need to visit any office in person?

Usually not. Patent, trademark, copyright, company, GST and most other filings are made online. We tell you if a particular matter needs anything in person.
