FAQ

Business & Tax: common questions

Which business structure is best for a startup in India?

A Private Limited Company usually suits startups that plan to raise equity investment or offer ESOPs. An LLP suits professional and owner-run businesses that want limited liability with lower compliance. The right choice depends on your plans.

Learn more

How many directors are needed for a private limited company?

A private limited company needs at least two directors and two shareholders (the same people can be both), and at least one director must be resident in India. A One Person Company needs only one member.

Learn more

Who is eligible for DPIIT Startup India recognition?

A private limited company, LLP or registered partnership firm that is up to 10 years old, has not had turnover above ₹100 crore in any financial year, and is working on innovation or a scalable business model can apply.

Learn more

When is GST registration mandatory?

GST registration is generally required when aggregate turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh for goods or ₹20 lakh for services in most states, and regardless of turnover in some cases, such as inter-state taxable supply of goods and most e-commerce sales.

Learn more

Is MSME (Udyam) registration free?

Yes. There is no government fee for Udyam registration on the official portal. Professional assistance is optional.

Learn more

What annual compliance does a private limited company have?

A private limited company must hold board meetings and an AGM, get its accounts audited, file financial statements (AOC-4) and its annual return (MGT-7 or MGT-7A) with the Registrar of Companies, and file its income tax return, among other filings.

Learn more
