FAQ

Patent deadlines: common questions

When must the complete specification be filed after a provisional application?

Within 12 months from the date of filing the provisional application (Section 9(1), Patents Act, 1970). Otherwise the application is treated as abandoned.

Learn more

What is the deadline for the request for examination in India?

Under the Patents (Amendment) Rules, 2024, the request for examination is due within 31 months from the priority date. Applications filed before 15 March 2024 were subject to a 48-month period; check the rule that applies to your application.

Learn more

When is the PCT national phase deadline for India?

National phase entry in India is due within 31 months from the priority date. Most other countries have a 30- or 31-month deadline.

Learn more

How long do I have to reply to a First Examination Report?

Six months from the date of the FER, extendable by up to three months on request.
