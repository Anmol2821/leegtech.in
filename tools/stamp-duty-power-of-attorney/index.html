FAQ

Power of Attorney stamp duty: common questions

Why do I need a Power of Attorney for a patent or trademark filing?

When an agent files or prosecutes an application on your behalf, the Patent Office or Trade Marks Registry needs your authorisation (Form 26 for patents, TM-48 for trade marks). That authorisation is a power of attorney and generally needs to be stamped as per the applicable state stamp law.

Learn more

What is the stamp duty on a Power of Attorney in Madhya Pradesh?

As listed by IP India: ₹2000/- Single Transaction: Authorizing one or more persons to act in a single transaction (other than registration). Check the linked state law for your situation.

Learn more

Does stamp duty depend on the state?

Yes. Stamp duty is a state subject, so the amount differs from state to state and can depend on the type of power of attorney (for example, for a single transaction or general). Always check the current state law before execution.
