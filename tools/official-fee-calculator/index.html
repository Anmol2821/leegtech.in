FAQ

Official fees: common questions

What is the official fee for a trademark application in India?

For e-filing, the official fee is ₹4,500 per class for individuals, startups and small enterprises, and ₹9,000 per class for all other applicants (Form TM-A).

Learn more

What is the official fee for filing a patent application in India?

For e-filing, the application fee (Form 1) is ₹1,600 for natural persons, startups, small entities and educational institutions, and ₹8,000 for others, for up to 30 sheets and 10 claims. The request for examination (Form 18) is ₹4,000 or ₹20,000.

Learn more

Who qualifies for the lower official fees?

Natural persons, DPIIT-recognised startups and small entities (and, for patents, educational institutions) pay the lower fees; for trademarks, individuals, startups and small enterprises. Eligibility must be supported with the prescribed proof, otherwise the higher fee applies.

Learn more

Do these amounts include professional fees?

No. This calculator shows official government fees only. Professional fees for drafting, filing and prosecution are separate and are quoted after a free consultation.
