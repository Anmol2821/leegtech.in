FAQ

Design: common questions

What is design registration in India?

Design registration protects the visual appearance of a product, such as its shape, configuration, pattern, ornamentation or composition of lines and colours, under the Designs Act, 2000. It does not protect how the product works.

Learn more

How long does design registration last in India?

A registered design is protected for 10 years from the date of registration and can be extended once by 5 more years, giving a maximum of 15 years.

Learn more

Can I register a design that is already on sale?

Generally no. A design must be new or original and not previously published in India or elsewhere, so you should file before any public disclosure, sale or online listing.

Learn more

What is the difference between design registration and copyright?

Design registration protects the appearance of an industrial article, while copyright protects artistic works. Copyright in a design that is capable of registration can be lost once the article is reproduced more than 50 times by an industrial process without registration, so registration is the safer route for products.

Learn more

Does India have utility models?

No. India does not currently have a utility model system. Technical inventions are protected by patents in India; utility models are available in countries such as Germany.

Learn more
