FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

What services does Leegtech provide?

Leegtech provides IP, business registration, compliance and legal documentation services: patents, trademarks, copyright and design registration, international filings (PCT, Madrid, US, UK), company and LLP registration, Startup India, MSME, GST, FSSAI, ISO, income tax and annual compliance, and drafting of agreements and legal notices.

Learn more

What is the difference between a patent, trademark, copyright and design?

A patent protects an invention or technical innovation, a trademark protects your brand identity, copyright protects original creative works, and design registration protects the visual appearance of a product. One product can be protected by more than one of them.

Learn more

Does Leegtech guarantee that my patent, trademark or registration will be granted?

No. No one can guarantee a grant, registration or approval. Decisions are made by the relevant government authorities, such as the Indian Patent Office, the Trade Marks Registry or the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, based on the law and the facts of each case. We prepare and file your matter carefully and keep you informed at every stage, but the outcome and timelines are not in our control.

Learn more

Is Leegtech a government office or affiliated with IP India or MCA?

No. Leegtech is a private company and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any government office. You can file most applications directly on the official government portals yourself. Our professional fees are for our assistance and are separate from official government fees, which are charged at actuals.

Learn more

Is this website an advertisement for legal services?

No. This website provides general information about our services and is not a solicitation or advertisement of legal services. Using the website or submitting an enquiry does not create a lawyer–client relationship. Where the law requires a step to be handled by an advocate or a registered patent or trademark agent, it is handled only by a person who is so qualified.

Learn more

Are government fees included in your fees?

No. Official government fees are charged at actuals and are shown separately from our professional fees in your quote. Reduced official fees apply to many startups, small entities, MSMEs and individuals, and we check your eligibility.

Learn more

Can Leegtech help with international IP protection?

Yes. We assist with PCT patent applications, USA and UK patent applications, UK design registration, German utility models, Madrid Protocol trademark applications and copyright registration in Canada.

Learn more

When should I file a patent or design application?

File before you publicly disclose, launch, exhibit or sell the invention or design. A public disclosure before filing can destroy novelty, and early filing secures your priority date.

Learn more

Can you help me reply to a patent or trademark objection?

Yes. We prepare replies to patent examination reports (FER) and trademark examination reports, and support hearings, amendments and opposition proceedings. Deadlines are strict: one month for a trademark examination report and six months (extendable by three) for a patent FER.

Learn more

Can you register my company or startup?

Yes. We assist with Private Limited Company, LLP, OPC and Section 8 company registration, DPIIT Startup India recognition and MSME Udyam registration.

Learn more

Can I protect several aspects of the same product?

Yes. A patent can protect the technology, design registration the appearance, a trademark the brand, and copyright the creative content such as software code and artwork.

Learn more

How do I get started with Leegtech?

Book a free consultation online, call or WhatsApp us. We review your requirement, explain the options, process and indicative timelines, and send a written fee quote before any work begins.
